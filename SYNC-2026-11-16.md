# Fourth sync, later on 2026-11-16: Home page responsive fix

Only `customizer/additional-css.css` changed. It is exported from the live
site; sha1 `535d3031636727021bc06898e1dac85aae05cdbb`, 122,896 bytes.

Two new blocks at the end of the file, both scoped to Home:

| Width | Fault | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 500–767px | ~990px of blank space under the "Where we work" cards | Cards had `min-height` plus `aspect-ratio: 3/4`, which forced them wider than their column and made the grid reserve a single-column height. Now an explicit two-column grid with a fixed card height (340px up to 560px, 360px from 561px). |
| 768–1024px | Fourth "Where we work" card alone on a second row | Same cause. Now four columns with a fixed 320px card height. |
| 561–767px | Value and service cards one per row | Three across, as on tablet. |

Verified on the live site at 510, 560, 600, 768, 900, 1024 and 1440px:
no sideways scroll, no gap, and the next section follows the cards. Phones
under 500px and desktop are unchanged.
